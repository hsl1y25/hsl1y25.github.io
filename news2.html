News PS5 Update Is Out Now

Sony had released the latest update for PlayStation 5 on 22 October 2025. PS5 owners can download the latest version of update--Version: 25.07-12.20.00.

The full update notes from Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC are as below:

Version: 25.07-12.20.00

  • You can now use your console to check the serial number of your console, and of DualSense wireless controllers that are currently connected by USB or Bluetooth®.
    • For your console, select Settings > System > System Software > Console Information, and then check Serial Number.
    • For a DualSense wireless controller, select Settings > Accessories > General > Advanced Settings > View Serial Number.
  • We've updated the device software of the DualSense Edge wireless controller to improve stability.
  • We've improved the messages and usability on some screens.
  • We've improved system software performance and stability.

Source: Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC

This version is a little bit more extensive than normal. According to the statement, the biggest update is there is a new feature that allows users to check the serial number of their console and DualSense wireless controller via the settings menu.

After this, a larger update for the PS5 might be released within this year, since Sony released its biggest update for the PS5 in the final months of each year previously.
