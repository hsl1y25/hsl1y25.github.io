Stardew Valley Creator Announces Version 1.7 Update

Stardew Valley creator ConcernedApe had announced Stardew Valley version 1.7 in August, he just simply said it is happening but did not provide any other details.

The full post from ConcernedApe is as below:

Okay, I revealed this at the concert last night and now the cat's out of the bag, so I will confirm for everyone: There will be a Stardew Valley 1.7 update. No release date, no estimate. But it's happening (Source: ConcernedApe on X, 31 August 2025)

After about a month, he responded to the fans and said that he does not want "too much hype at this point" and he also said that "it is going to be a while before the update is ready". Below is the post:

more will come. I just don't want too much hype at this point. It's going to be a while before the update is ready (Source: ConcernedApe on X, 22 September 2025)

Thus, Stardew fans might need to wait for a while for the update.
