Elden Ring For Switch 2 Delayed To 2026

Elden Ring Switch 2 is not going to release on time. Originally, the plan was to release the game in 2025, but it has been delayed due to "performance adjustment", according to the statement from FromSoftware.

The full statement from FromSoftware is as below:

The Nintendo Switch 2 game "Elden Ring Tarnished Edition", originally scheduled for release in 2025, will now be released in 2026, as we have determined that we need more time to adjust the game's performance. We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this may cause to users who have been waiting for the release of this title. We will continue to make adjustments to deliver the game in the best possible form, and we appreciate your understanding. Thank you for your patience. (Source: FromSoftware on X)

According to the announcement, FromSoftware needs some time to make performance adjustments. FromSoftware apologised for that and thanked users for their understanding.

Previously, Elden Ring has been playable on Nintendo Switch 2. However, people who played Elden Ring on Switch 2 are not getting a good gaming experience due to its inconsistent frame rate.

For information, Elden Ring is a 2022 action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Elden Ring has won "Game of the Year", "Best Game Direction", "Best Art Direction" and "Best Role Playing Game" awards in The Game Awards 2022.
