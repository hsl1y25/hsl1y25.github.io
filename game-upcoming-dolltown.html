Welcome to Doll Town

About The Game

Once a bustling village known for its exquisite handcrafted dolls, Doll Town now rots in silence and shadows. Abandoned factories, empty schools, and forgotten shrines are all that remain—haunted by memories of the students who perished here years ago.

You play as Yumi, a student who takes a part-time job at the town’s only convenience store, unknowingly stepping into a nightmare that refuses to stay buried. As the line between reality and dream dissolves, Yumi must uncover the truth behind the deaths, the cursed dolls, and the macabre love story that destroyed the town.

Source: Steam
